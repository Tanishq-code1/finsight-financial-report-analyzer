Extract key financial performance indicators and qualitative disclosures from the provided financial report excerpts into structured JSON.

REQUIRED METRICS IN SCOPE:
- Revenue / Total Net Sales
- Operating Income / Operating Profit
- Net Income
- Operating Cash Flow
- Capital Expenditures (Capex)
- Cash and Cash Equivalents
- Total Debt / Borrowings
- Diluted EPS

EXTRACTION RULES:
1. Preserve exact currency, magnitude scale (thousands, millions, billions), and period labels (e.g. FY2024, FY2023).
2. Never represent a missing metric as zero. If not stated in the excerpts, mark status as 'not_found' with null value.
3. Every found metric MUST include the 1-based page number and a concise verbatim quote from the text where it appears.
4. Qualitative Risks: Extract top disclosed risks with stated mitigations where available. Do not invent severity levels.
5. Management Outlook: Extract forward-looking statements and guidance, noting the target period.
